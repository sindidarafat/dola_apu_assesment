Case work: from diagnosis to a costed plan

Both cases are from Bizception 2026, a marketing case competition run by the Dhaka University Career Club and presented by Bashundhara Exercise Book. I led team One-Zero with Istiak Shuvo. Each case follows the same method: find the root problem, split the customers, position the product, then put numbers and KPIs behind the plan.

Round 1 · Exercise book case

Bashundhara Khata: Recognize the Original

The problem

Look-alike notebooks copy the red-white trade dress, and shopkeepers pass them off as genuine or swap in a generic khata for margin. Advertising lifts the category but not the brand, and a BDT 20 product from Babubazar threatens the BDT 50 line.

The insight

Two buyers share one shelf. Guardians, parents of 5–10 year olds, buy on trust. Autonomous students aged 16–24 buy on price. The sale is decided in the three seconds a shopkeeper picks which khata to hand over, so the plan needs consumer pull and retailer push.

The plan: three pillars

  • Bijoy Card. Retailer gamification. A unique QR on every case, a WhatsApp bot with no app download, bronze to platinum tiers, and a back-end rebate that matches Babubazar's trade margin without cutting shelf price.
  • Asol Cheno Seal. Three ways to verify, even offline: scan a serialized QR, send a missed call or SMS, or feel a die-cut corner notch.
  • Segmented campaigns. A trust story for guardians and a fair-price, identity story for students.

Round 2 · Ice cream case

Polar Ice Cream: Chill Together

The problem

Winter sales fall 65% while SMP, AMF and cocoa costs rise 22%. Load-shedding melts stock, retailers push whoever offers the better scheme, and Polar sits in the squeezed middle between Igloo above and low-margin challengers below.

The insight

Bangladeshis don't stop loving dessert in winter. It is the most social season and ice cream is missing from its moments. Winter is won on occasion and premium, not on discounts.

The plan: five moves, one idea

  • Dessert for every season. A Winter Heritage Line with Nolen Gur Crunch, Cinnamon Cha and Pitha Swirl, plus share packs and a Dessert Box.
  • Polar Safe Zones. Phase-change material plates on 10,000 high-risk freezers, so stock survives outages. Retrofitting beats new freezers, which carry a 104% import duty.
  • Polar Partner Programme. Gold and Platinum tiers scored on sales growth only and paid in free stock.
  • #ChillTogether creator engine. 346 creators from national to district level.
  • Chill Together Adda. A five-city Winter Dessert Festival, campus and wedding events, and a year-round media plan.

The deck also stress-tests the plan: a +15% winter lift returns 9% ROI with payback in Month 32, and a +50% lift returns 84% with payback in Month 16.

What this shows for an analyst role

Problem framing

Both decks open with a root-cause diagnosis that ties every proposed move back to a named problem.

Customer segmentation

Guardians and students in Round 1, weekend families, young pros, campus crews and B2B partners in Round 2, each with its own need and channel.

Business case

Costed budgets, an ROI and payback model, and sensitivity scenarios instead of a single optimistic number.

KPIs and risk

Measurable targets for every move, and a likelihood, impact, mitigation and Plan B table for each risk.
